# Design Review (GStack / UX-UI Agent Standard)

Comprehensive multi-breakpoint design audit and heuristic evaluation.

## Review Criteria
1. **Nielsen Norman Heuristics:**
   - Visibility of system status
   - Match between system and the real world (botanical beginner terminology)
   - User control and freedom (undo, confirmation, recovery)
   - Consistency and standards (icons, buttons, card borders)
   - Error prevention and recovery
   - Recognition rather than recall
   - Flexibility and efficiency of use
   - Aesthetic and minimalist design
2. **Responsive Breakpoints:**
   - Mobile Small / Standard: 375px - 414px
   - Tablet: 768px
   - Desktop: 1280px - 1440px
3. **Touch Targets & Hit Areas:** Minimum 44x44px for primary interactions.
